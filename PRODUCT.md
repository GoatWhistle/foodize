# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two equally important audiences:

**Customers** — students and office workers placing food pre-orders. On mobile, mid-task, looking to order quickly and pick up without waiting. Their primary job: find what they want, confirm, and move on.

**Vendors/Restaurants** — owners and staff managing menus, orders, and operations. On desktop or tablet, during a shift. Their primary job: see incoming orders fast and keep inventory accurate.

## Product Purpose

Foodize is a food pre-ordering platform connecting customers with local restaurants. Customers browse menus, build carts, and place timed pickup orders. Vendors manage their listings, receive orders, and track fulfillment. The platform also supports staff role management and a Telegram Mini App integration.

Success looks like: customers feel confident the food will be ready when they arrive. Vendors feel in control of their workflow.

## Brand Personality

Warm, deliberate, premium.

Not corporate warmth — the warmth of a good independent restaurant: quality-forward, attentive, a little special. Think Deliveroo circa 2019, not Uber Eats.

## Anti-references

- **Яндекс Еда / Delivery Club** — overloaded UI, aggressive discount banners, corporate red.
- **Wolt** — blue Scandinavian minimalism, cold and clinical.
- **Uber Eats** — dark mode with neon green, aggressive typography, discount-first hierarchy.
- **Generic SaaS** — Tailwind clones with identical card grids, Inter on white, no personality.

## Design Principles

1. **Appetite before architecture** — every screen should make food look desirable before it handles logistics. Visual hierarchy leads with product, not UI chrome.
2. **Speed for vendors, confidence for customers** — customers need assurance (order confirmed, time locked); vendors need immediacy (new order, what's in it, now).
3. **Earned warmth** — the fire/amber palette and serif accents earn their place through restraint. Not every element is warm; the contrast makes warmth meaningful.
4. **Platform, not portal** — Foodize connects two sides of a transaction. Design should reflect partnership, not a delivery app that tolerates restaurant listings.
5. **Mobile-first, not mobile-only** — customers are on phones; vendors are on screens. Both experiences must be complete, not compromises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Sufficient color contrast on all text and interactive elements. No color-only status signals (always pair with text or icon). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
